I'm proud to announce that, on November 9th, I successfully defended my PhD Thesis, entitled ***Nanophotonic chirality and optical forces: sensing and manipulation at the nanoscale***, receiving the highest distinction, _Sobresaliente cum laude_.

This milestone marks the completion of my doctoral studies and the end of my time at the University of Cantabria. I am also glad to announce that I will soon be joining Alberto G. Curto's lab at the Photonics Research Group at Ghent University as a postdoctoral researcher, where I will continue my research on chiral nanophotonics.
